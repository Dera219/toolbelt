Questions

Straight answers

When am I actually charged?

Your card is authorized when you accept a worker's offer, which places a hold but does not take the money. The charge happens only when the job is marked complete. If the job is cancelled beforehand, the hold is released.

What if the work isn't done properly?

Do not confirm completion. Raise it in the app and we will review the job record and messages. Refunds, full or partial, are issued through the platform.

Who are the workers?

Independent local contractors, not ToolBelt employees. Each passes identity and eligibility vetting and verifies a phone number before taking work.

Why can't I book an electrician or plumber?

Those trades require licensing, and we do not list them until license verification is built. Listing an unlicensed worker for regulated work would put customers at risk.

How do workers get paid?

Payouts go to the worker's bank account after the job is confirmed complete, minus the 15% service fee. Payments and payouts are processed by Stripe.
